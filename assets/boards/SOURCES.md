# Sources for the board images in `assets/boards/`

Every image here is an operator-supplied illustration or render of the board it
names, made for this project's BIRTH board picker and node pages. They are
released under the repository's licence (MIT, see `LICENSE`). Vendor names and
logos visible in a render (Raspberry Pi, Heltec, LilyGO, RAKwireless, Seeed,
Ebyte) appear only to identify the hardware being flashed — a nominative use;
the marks belong to their owners and no endorsement is implied.
