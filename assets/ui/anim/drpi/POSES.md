# Dr. Pi — pose sheet

Ten poses of the operator's Dr. Pi character, drawn 2026-08-04 and cut from a
plain white sheet. Background removed by edge flood fill; the cut is exact
because the backdrop was near-pure white behind dark linework.

(An earlier attempt to lift him out of the full scene frames FAILED and was
discarded: there his cream coat sat against a cream wall, only 48 channel-units
apart, while crossing the wall's own gradient needed a tolerance spanning 36.
No threshold separated them. If more poses are ever needed, draw them on plain
white or a colour that appears nowhere in the art.)
